# Project Provenance

## Original authorship and provenance controls

The AI/ML Engineering Framework was conceived, structured and originally authored
by Iain Clark, who remains the author and maintainer of the core framework. 

Iain Clark acknowledges the use of AI assistance in drafting and developing 
technical material; responsibility for technical judgement, review, verification 
and approval remains with the author and maintainer.

This project provenance statement was proposed by an external reviewer who wishes 
to remain anonymous, with assistance from ChatGPT, and has been gratefully incorporated 
into the framework by Iain Clark following maintainer review and minor edits.

## External review

External reviewers may identify gaps, propose ideas, challenge assumptions
or recommend sources. Review comments do not constitute authorship of the
framework unless repository content is directly authored by the reviewer
and accepted as a contribution.

## Adoption of review comments

The maintainer independently assesses external feedback and decides whether,
how and when it should be incorporated. Material changes are recorded through
the repository's commit and decision history.

## Direct contributions

Directly authored contributions are attributed through Git history, pull
requests and the contributor acknowledgement record.

## AI assistance

Where generative AI materially assists drafting, analysis or review, the
nature of that assistance and the responsible human reviewer are recorded.
Human maintainers remain responsible for technical decisions, verification
and approval.

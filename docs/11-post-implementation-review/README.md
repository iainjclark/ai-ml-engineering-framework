# 11 --- Post-Implementation Review

> **What actually happened in operation, and what did we learn?**

## Purpose

Compare what happened in operation with what was intended, capture lessons, and turn operational evidence into better future decisions.

## When Should It Be Created

Create this after enough operational use exists to judge outcomes, and after major releases, incidents or other useful review points.

## When Should It Be Updated

Update it when further operational evidence, incidents, drift, user feedback or completed improvement actions change the findings.

## Inputs

Use operational results, monitoring, incidents, user feedback, business outcomes, original objectives, requirements, risks and release records.

## Activities

Compare actual outcomes with expectations. Investigate gaps, successes and failures, identify lessons, and agree improvement actions.

## Outputs / Artefacts

Record findings, lessons and improvement actions, including links to new requirements, risks, decisions or changes where needed.

## Traceability

-   Link important operational findings and lessons to the requirements,
    risks, decisions, evidence and changes they affect.
-   Compare actual operational outcomes with the concept-stage
    objectives, stakeholder needs, requirements and expected benefits.
-   Link incidents, defects, drift, control failures and unexpected
    behaviour to the affected configuration, risks, requirements and
    design elements.
-   Link improvement actions to subsequent decisions, change records and
    new or revised requirements.
-   Preserve evidence of both successful outcomes and shortcomings so
    that lessons can inform later releases and projects.
-   The purpose is to show **whether the system delivered the intended
    outcome in operation and how operational evidence changed subsequent
    engineering decisions**.
